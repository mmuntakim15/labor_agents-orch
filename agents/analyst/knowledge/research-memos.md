# Research memos (accounting research and judgment recipes)

A research request (group 5: contract reviews, capitalization, intercompany recharges, tax compliance status, policy drafts) ends in a memo for an accountant who signs the conclusion. The desk prepares; the judgement stays with the accountant.

## What the memo is
- Draft `memo.md` in the artifacts folder and deliver it as a pdf with `export.js pdf`; the markdown is never the deliverable. Figures that exist (contract amounts, costs by project, recharges, threshold tests) also go in a workbook with the standard Exceptions, Tie-out, Detail and Sources sheets.
- Fixed sections, in this order: Facts (from the documents, each with the clause or page), Guidance (the paragraphs relied on), Alternatives (each with the facts it depends on), Missing facts, Preliminary conclusion.
- Short sentences. No em-dashes. Plain words an accountant uses; no tool, route or receipt words.

## Citing guidance
- Cite the paragraph (for example ASC 606-10-25-14, ASC 842-10-15-3, ASC 350-40-25-2), not the topic alone.
- Mark every citation "verify": the desk has no codification subscription and the accountant checks the paragraph before relying on it. Write it as `ASC 606-10-25-14 (verify)`.
- Quote the contract or the policy, never paraphrase a term that drives the accounting (payment timing, termination rights, variable consideration, renewal options).

## No conclusion where facts are missing
- When a fact the alternative depends on is not in the documents, say which fact is missing and stop there. "Cannot be concluded without X" is the right sentence; a guess dressed as a conclusion is not.
- Never use definitely, certainly or guaranteed. Prefer "the facts support", "appears to", "would need", "depends on".
- The preliminary conclusion names the alternative the facts support best and the facts that would change it. It is addressed to the sign-off owner from setup when one is given.
- Draft journal entries, when the memo needs them, live inside the memo as tables and are never proposed in Dime. Recharges, allocations and threshold tests show their arithmetic from the figures pulled.

## What goes in work.md
- Each document read (name, pages, what it contained, anything odd).
- Each citation and why it applies.
- Each missing fact and where the desk looked for it.
- Receipts for every figure in the workbook.
